# GAIL Pipeline Agent — "One Morning at GAIL" (V3)

A Gemini Enterprise agent built with ADK on Vertex AI Agent Engine. In one conversation it:

1. **Accesses data:** SCADA history, GIS grid and customer nominations from the GCS data lake.
2. **Runs deterministic model #1:** LNG landed-cost comparison with a delivery-time constraint.
3. **Runs deterministic model #2:** a real SARIMAX line-pack forecast, fitted live, with a with/without-action scenario.
4. **Produces a report and an action:** a one-page decision brief plus an HTML briefing on GCS, and a staged SAP work order.

The on-stage script is in **[DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md)**. Engineering notes are in **[BUILD.md](BUILD.md)**.

> [!NOTE]
> Nominations, SCADA history and LNG prices are illustrative, generated by `fixtures/generate_fixtures.py`. The models are real.

## Demo flow (≈ 8 minutes, one Gemini Enterprise chat)

**The idea to land:** *any data → any model → any report → action, in one conversation. Shown on gas; the audience swaps in their own work.*
The one-page presenter crib sheet is in **[PRESENTER_CRIB_SHEET.md](PRESENTER_CRIB_SHEET.md)**.

### Opening (no screen)
> "You know this business far better than I do. I'll show you a pattern using a gas example.
> It's 6 AM. Customers revised tomorrow's orders. Three questions before the 9 AM call: **Can we supply it? What's the cheapest gas? Will the pipeline hold?** Watch one conversation do it."

### Beat 1: Problem (data access)
- **Paste:** `Show me today's grid flows and tomorrow's customer nominations.`
- **Appears:** Grid-flow card + ⚠️ **4.0 MMSCMD shortfall from 08:00** (Fertilizer +3.0, CGD +1.01).
- **Say:** "It joined SCADA, GIS and commercial nominations, and found a gap nobody asked it to look for."
- **Swap for yours:** "Finance: your ledger plus the bank feed. HR: rosters against leave."

### Beat 2: Decision (deterministic model #1)
- **Paste:** `What's the cheapest way to cover that shortfall?`
- **Appears:** Delivered-cost bars at Dahej: Qatar swap **$11.83** (recommended), spot $14.45 (dearer), Henry Hub $10.02 (too late: 34 days vs 7 days of cover). **₹70.3 Cr saved.**
- **Say:** "The cheapest option on paper arrives too late. It enforced that constraint with fixed formulas, not guesswork."
- **Swap for yours:** "Procurement: bids against delivery dates."

### Beat 3: Proof (deterministic model #2, SARIMAX)
- **Paste:** `Can the grid carry it? Forecast Chhainsa for 24 hours.`
- **Appears:** A five-step model workflow (load 72h → detect daily cycle → fit SARIMAX → back-test 0.33% → forecast), then one chart:
  - navy = last 48h of actual pressure (daily pack/draft cycle)
  - "Now" marker
  - **red** dashed line with 80%/95% cones = without action → **breach at 19:00**
  - **green** dashed line with cones = with the LNG swap → stays ≥ **79.53** (even the 95% band clears the 76 floor)
- **Say (left to right):** "On the left is what actually happened. It learned that pattern and proved it by predicting yesterday within a third of a percent. On the right, red is tonight if we do nothing and green is tonight if we act. A forecast tells you what will happen; this tells you what happens **if you act**."
- **Swap for yours:** "Any forecast you run in Excel: demand, cash, manpower, maintenance."

### Beat 4: Action (report + system of record)
- **Paste:** `Brief management and raise the SAP order.`
- **Appears:** "Decision briefed and actioned" card, a **link to the HTML decision brief** (one-page decision box on top), and a staged SAP work-order ID.
- **Say:** "One page for the MD. The SAP order is staged for a human to approve, not auto-posted."
- **Swap for yours:** "Your MIS, your board note, your audit pack."

### Close
> "Four questions, one conversation. Nothing here was gas-specific except the data. **What's the 6 AM question on your desk?**"
